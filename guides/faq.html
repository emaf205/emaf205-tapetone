TAPETONE FAQ

Short answers about the static web app.

Does it work offline?

When installed or previously cached as a PWA, the core app can work offline. Direct local HTML use also works, although PWA installation requires HTTPS or localhost.

Are audio files uploaded?

The audio processing logic is client-side. The app does not intentionally upload your audio to an audio-processing service.

What formats are supported?

WAV and MP3 are accepted when the browser can decode the file.
