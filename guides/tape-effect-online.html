EMAF205 / TAPETONE GUIDE

Free Online Tape Effect

TAPETONE adds tape-style saturation, movement and filtering directly in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Upload WAV or MP3.
  2. Choose Tape, Cassette, Boombox or Recorder.
  3. Choose New, Worn or Dead.
  4. Press Process, compare Original and FX, then export WAV.

Local audio processing

The audio effect runs in the browser. The static app does not require an external mastering API.
